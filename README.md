## 1. Problem Definition & Business Understanding

**Problem.** A telecom operator loses revenue every time a subscriber cancels. Acquiring a new customer costs
far more than retaining one, but retention offers cannot be sent to everyone.

**Objective.** Predict which customers are likely to churn so the retention team can target them with offers.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Stakeholders | Retention/CRM team, marketing, finance, customer-support leads |
| Business relevance | Fewer lost customers, retention budget spent where it matters |
| ML task | Binary classification (`churn` = Yes/No) |
| Model KPIs | ROC-AUC, recall on churners, precision, F1, PR-AUC |
| Business KPI | Expected cost = FN x 500 + FP x 50 (assumed: value of a lost customer vs cost of one retention offer) |

## 2. Dataset & Data Understanding

7,045 raw rows x 18 columns (7,000 unique customers after de-duplication). Full quality report: `outputs/data_quality_summary.csv`.

| Column | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|
| customer_id | ID | Unique customer identifier (dropped before modelling) |
| gender, senior_citizen, partner, dependents | demographic | Customer profile |
| tenure_months | numeric | Months with the company (1-72) |
| phone_service, internet_service | service | Phone yes/no; Fiber optic / DSL / No |
| online_security, tech_support, streaming | service | Yes / No / No internet service |
| contract | categorical | Month-to-month / One year / Two year |
| paperless_billing, payment_method | billing | Billing preferences |
| monthly_charges, total_charges | numeric | Current monthly bill; cumulative billed amount |
| support_calls | numeric | Support calls in recent period |
| **churn** | **target** | Yes = customer left (33.8% of customers) |

Data quality issues found: 45 duplicate rows, 70 missing `payment_method`, 92 missing `total_charges`, 21 extreme `monthly_charges` outliers.

## 3. Data Preprocessing & Cleaning

| Issue | Treatment | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Duplicate rows | Removed (45) | Duplicates inflate evidence and leak across train/test |
| Missing numeric | Median imputation | Robust to skew |
| Missing categorical | Most-frequent imputation | Keeps rows, small share missing |
| Outliers in `monthly_charges` | IQR rule, capped at 135.6 (winsorised) | Keeps customers, removes impossible bills (`figures/01_outlier_treatment.png`) |
| Categorical variables | One-hot encoding (`handle_unknown="ignore"`) | Models need numeric input |
| Numeric scaling | StandardScaler | Needed for logistic regression |

All imputers/encoders/scalers sit **inside a scikit-learn `Pipeline`** and are fitted on the training split only.

## 4. Exploratory Data Analysis

Figures in `figures/`.

- **Class balance:** 33.8% churn - moderately imbalanced, so `class_weight="balanced"` and PR-AUC are used.
- **Contract is the strongest driver.** Month-to-month customers churn far more than annual/two-year ones (chi-square = 1144.7, p < 0.001).
- **Tenure:** churners have a median tenure of 23 months vs 38 for loyal customers (Mann-Whitney p < 0.001).
- **Price:** churners pay more (median 75.7 vs 64.5 per month, p < 0.001); fibre customers churn most.
- **Add-ons help retention:** customers with tech support or online security churn less.
- **Gender is not related to churn** (chi-square p = 0.45) - correctly excluded from the story.

## 5. Feature Engineering / Selection

| Feature | Logic |
|---|---|
| `tenure_group` | Bins 0-12, 13-24, 25-48, 49-72 months |
| `is_new_customer` | Tenure <= 6 months |
| `high_support_user` | 3+ support calls |
| `n_protection_services` | Count of security + tech support add-ons |
| `month_to_month_fiber` | Interaction of the two riskiest attributes |

**Leakage/redundancy handling:** `customer_id` dropped (identifier). `total_charges` dropped because it is approximately
`monthly_charges x tenure` (redundant and collinear). The train/test split happens **before** fitting any transformer.

## 6. Model Selection & Implementation

- Stratified 80/20 split (5,600 train / 1,400 test), 5-fold stratified CV on the training set.
- Three models of increasing complexity, all as full pipelines: Logistic Regression (interpretable baseline),
  Random Forest, Gradient Boosting.

| Model | CV AUC | Test AUC | PR-AUC | Precision | Recall | F1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Logistic Regression** | 0.825 | **0.830** | 0.695 | 0.603 | 0.772 | 0.677 |
| Random Forest | 0.817 | 0.822 | 0.684 | 0.597 | 0.717 | 0.651 |
| Gradient Boosting | 0.817 | 0.821 | 0.679 | 0.661 | 0.581 | 0.619 |

The simple, interpretable logistic regression performs as well as the tree ensembles, so it is selected (Occam's razor,
easy to explain to the business). Hyper-parameter search was deliberately kept out of scope; it is a natural extension.

## 7. Model Evaluation & Validation

Accuracy alone is misleading on imbalanced data, so evaluation uses a confusion matrix, ROC and PR curves
(`figures/07_confusion_matrices.png`, `figures/08_roc_pr_curves.png`).

| Threshold | TN | FP | FN | TP | Precision | Recall | Total cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.50 (default) | 687 | 240 | 108 | 365 | 0.60 | 0.77 | 66,000 |
| **0.16 (cost-optimal)** | 325 | 602 | 13 | 460 | 0.43 | 0.97 | **36,600** |

Moving the threshold cuts the assumed cost by about 45%, at the price of contacting many more customers who would have stayed.
The cost ratio (10:1) is an **assumption**; the business should replace it with real figures. Permutation importance
(`figures/09_feature_importance.png`) ranks `contract` and `tenure_months` far above everything else.

## 8. Business Insights & Recommendations

1. **Target the high-risk band.** Customers scored High (probability > 0.6) actually churned 64% of the time vs 8% in the Low band (`outputs/risk_band_summary.csv`).
2. **Offer contract upgrades.** Month-to-month + fibre is the riskiest segment (61% churn, 1,684 customers). Discounts for moving to a 1-2 year contract address the biggest driver.
3. **Protect new customers.** Run an onboarding programme for customers in their first 12 months.
4. **Bundle tech support / online security** as free trials - they are associated with lower churn (association, not proven causation).
5. **Escalate repeated support callers** (3+ calls) to a dedicated retention agent.
6. **Review fibre pricing** - higher bills and fibre service both correlate with churn.

**Limitations:** synthetic data; correlation is not causation; cost figures are assumed; no hyper-parameter tuning; no time dimension.
